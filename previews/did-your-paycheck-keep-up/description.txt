In September 2026 US prices jumped, led by fuel, and real pay fell compared with a year earlier. Is this the moment paychecks fell behind, or one bad year in a longer story?

We compare a quarter century of consumer prices, average hourly pay and house prices from official public data, and give our honest take on what it means for your money.

HOW WE DID THE MATHS
We rebase prices, hourly pay and house prices to 100 in January 2000 so they can share one chart, and compare the median new-home price with hourly pay to count the hours of work a home costs.
